# Model of NodeBox Live

NodeBox Live uses a different model from traditional applications such as Photoshop or Illustrator.

Imagine an **assembly line** of workers.

Each worker does one thing, and one thing only. After he or she has done their operation, the object is passed on to the next person who does his thing.

Other applications suchs as pure data, vvvv and blender have a similar approach.

## Functions.

In the NodeBox Live assembly line **functions** are the workers. They can be represented as

- `interconnectable nodes`.
- `pieces of JavaScript code`.

Functions are the **principle primitives** of NodeBox Live and are the basic building blocks of the application. Both can be used interchangeably.

More complex projects are often composed of multiple functions. By combining functions, you create your own assembly line where f.i. a new shape goes in and a complex result comes out. Every step along the way can be changed and viewed.

The [function list](guide:function-list) stores all project related functions ([dependencies](guide:dependencies) as well as user functions).
