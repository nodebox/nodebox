# Geometry

NodeBox Live can perform two-dimensional math using points and lines.

### Creating points

[makePoint](ref:g.makePoint) — make a point based on X/Y values.

### Calculations on points

[angle](ref:g.angle) — calculate an angle between two points.

[coordinates](ref:g.coordinates) — calculate a point based on another point.

[distance](ref:g.distance) — calculate the distance between two points.

[reflect](ref:g.reflect) — reflect a point over an axis.

[mix](ref:g.mix) — interpolate between two points / colors / numbers.

### Geometric queries

[bounds](ref:g.bounds) — give the bounds of the shape.

[centerPoint](ref:g.centerPoint) — get the center of the shape.

### Shape to points

[scatterPoints](ref:g.scatterPoints) — scatter points within a shape.

[toPoints](ref:g.toPoints) — give the points that make up a shape.

[resample](ref:g.resample) — calculate new points on the shape.

[pointOnPath](ref:g.pointOnPath) — calculate a point on a path.
